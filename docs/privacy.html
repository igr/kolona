Privacy Policy

Čuvar Kolone for iPhone and Android · Last updated: October 27, 2026

Short version

Čuvar Kolone does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What the app keeps

The app saves a few settings on your device only: the language, the city, whether choosing a city also switches the language, and your best rescue in each city. The app never sends this data anywhere. Deleting the app deletes it.

Network

The app does not connect to the internet.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue on GitHub.
