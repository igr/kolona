Čuvar Kolone

Guardian of the March

A portrait action game for iPhone and Android. A protest march of 72 people walks through a real city, and you are its guardian. Tap the street to move, stop to raise a barricade, and get at least 36 people to the final square.

  • Three cities with real routes: Belgrade, Paris and Milan.
  • Thugs, police and cars come in waves. The march stops for two speeches.
  • A new map every game, with real street names, landmarks and street life.
  • Play in English, Serbian, French or Italian.
  • No ads, no accounts, no data collected.
